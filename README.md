# CivicTrace

**Digital Waste Intelligence for Connected Municipal Operations**

CivicTrace is a lightweight **digital identity and event layer** designed to make municipal waste activity traceable and connected. It assigns persistent digital identities to municipal waste assets and Bulk Waste Generators (BWGs), linking their **locations, events, history, and status** in one system. CivicTrace

## What It Does

CivicTrace connects ground-level activities such as:

-  Waste collection
-  Citizen reports
-  Inspections
-  Maintenance
-  Compliance events
-  Geographic activity

The platform uses **rule-based pattern detection** to surface recurring issues such as post-collection overflow, overdue collections, high-maintenance assets, and repeated BWG compliance concerns. CivicTrace

## Core Workflow

```text
Digital Identity
       ↓
    Events
       ↓
    History
       ↓
      GIS
       ↓
  Intelligence
       ↓
MCD Verification
       ↓
Existing MCD Processes
```

CivicTrace is designed to **support municipal decision-making, not replace it**. Detected patterns are review signals; authorised MCD officials verify cases and determine the appropriate action. CivicTrace

## Key Features

- **Municipal Asset Registry** — Persistent identities for waste assets such as `BIN-0037`
- **BWG Management** — Digital records for entities such as `BWG-0142`
- **QR-Based Workflows** — Record collections and submit citizen reports
- **Event History** — Connected chronological records for every entity
- **GIS Intelligence** — Geographic view of assets, BWGs, and issues
- **Rule-Based Analytics** — Explainable and auditable pattern detection
- **Attention Queue** — Prioritised cases requiring administrative review
- **MCD Verification Workflow** — Keeps enforcement decisions with authorised officials
- **Interactive Guided Demo** — Walks users through the complete CivicTrace workflow
